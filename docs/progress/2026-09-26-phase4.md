## Respawn and game end (item 9, Phase 6 decisions)

- Done:
  - A freed human's ranger leaves `Session.Humans` alone
    (`RemoveHuman`, `ClearLocalPlayer`); the other humans stay.
  - Respawn. Every human's death is recorded (`TShip.RecordHumanDeath`:
    the `PlayerDeath` event with `HumanId`). In multiplayer
    (`IsMultiplayer`: more than one human) the ranger is not freed at the
    end of the star's day: it leaves the film as a landing ship does, is
    repaired and lands at the nearest planet that takes it
    (`FindRespawnPlanet`, `RespawnHuman`), with everything it had. The
    human gets a bubble and its `PlayerStar` follows. Single player keeps
    the game over; the goldens did not move.
  - Script game end. In multiplayer `EndGame` ends the game for the
    script's human only (`FinishHuman`, `THuman.Finished` and
    `FinishReason`, saved in version 2 of the per-human block). A finished
    human's commands are refused.
  - `tools/test.py`: the 2- and 8-human runs destroy two rangers
    (`--sim-kill-human`) and check the respawn; a lost human and an
    accepted command from a finished human fail the test.
  - `HUMANS:` markers: 91 → 83 (death, respawn and script game end).
- Notes:
  - The respawn happens at the end of the star's day, not at the fatal
    hit, so the film shows the destruction and no code in the rest of
    the day sees a ranger that jumped away mid-step.
  - A respawn in another star goes through `ShipsInTransit`, like a jump.
    Like a jump, it moves the ranger before the galaxy job, which skips
    stars that hold a human: that day the old star runs again and the new
    star does not run.
  - The bubble text is `Multiplayer.Respawned` in the language data, with
    an English fallback, since the game's data has no such text.
  - Not handled: the planet-captured loss (`ApplyPlanetCaptured`) still
    sets the global game end reason; a human in a captured planet is not
    yet respawned. The local human's screens after a respawn in another
    star (the star view, the background) are untested; the jump screen's
    `ExecuteHyperspaceStep` is what the UI does after a star change.

## Humans as targets (item 8)

- Done:
  - One rule, `CanHumanAttack(Attacker, Target)` in `aHuman`: false only when
    two different humans' rangers meet and friendly fire is off. Missiles and
    tranclucators count as their owner. See [docs/humans.md](../humans.md),
    PvP, for every site.
  - Refused with friendly fire off: the attack commands (auto battle, pending
    follow, weapon and interceptor targets; a follow at weapon range becomes a
    plain follow), `CanSelectShipTarget`, the auto-battle target, the
    automatic-control AI's enemy and weapon choice, joint attacks, NPC requests
    to join an attack, interceptor targets. A weapon aimed at another human does
    not fire (the combat step drops the target), and hits, missiles,
    interceptors, script damage and explosions a human set off do no damage to
    another human.
  - The switch: the `SetSessionFlag` executor drops every refused attack at
    once (`DropRefusedHumanAttacks`), so all machines drop them at the same
    command. Turning it on restores nothing.
  - Consequences, relations and respawn needed no change: attacks and kills
    between humans already went through the ranger code (reaction, nearby
    ships' judgement, reputation, kill counts, `RecordHumanDeath`, respawn), and
    `JoinHuman` gives the new human the race defaults an NPC ranger gets.
  - `--sim-pvp`: the local human attacks a second human in its star with
    friendly fire on (damage) and off (orders gone and refused, no damage from
    the weapons or a direct hit). `tools/test.py` runs it with 2 and 8 humans,
    twice. Taking the rule out makes it fail.
  - Single-player goldens did not move. No `HUMANS:` marker was about this.
- Notes:
  - Only a human's ranger is protected; its tranclucator and its NPC partner
    are not.
  - The test's target has no orders after takeoff; it does not fight back.
  - Hot-seat and Phase 5: the flag command must come only from the host; the
    executor does not check who sent it. The star map hides the attack cursor
    for another human only through `CanSelectShipTarget`; a client must read
    the flag from the snapshot, which the per-human save block already carries.

## End-turn barrier and hot-seat (item 2's last part, test plan, Milestone 3)

- Done:
  - The barrier. `EndTurn` (kind 43) marks the actor's `THuman.EndedTurn`;
    `HumanHoldsDay` / `AllHumansEndedTurn` / `NextHumanToEndTurn` in `aHuman`.
    Finished humans and humans in hyperspace never hold the day; Phase 5 adds
    disconnected ones there. Every UI place where an end turn starts the day
    asks `aPlayerActions.EndTurn` right before the player-star job (star map end
    turn and automatic continue, planet/station panel, the three takeoffs, the
    jump screen). The player-star job starts the next round (`StartDayRound`).
    With one human it always passes; single-player goldens did not move.
  - Continue until arrival: in multiplayer the star map's automatic continue
    sends `EndTurn` with the flag; `HumanTravelContinues` (the star map's test
    without the local-screen parts) keeps the human ended while travel goes on.
    The break button ends it (`StopContinuing`, kind 45).
  - Non-local humans' hyperspace steps: due after the player-star job, run at
    the galaxy job's start (`StepHumansInHyperspace`, multiplayer only).
  - `JoinHuman` is a command (kind 44), so logs replay joins.
  - Hot-seat UI (`source/screens/HotSeat.pas`, `--hotseat=N`): the screen
    passes to the first human who holds the day, with a notice; the switch
    (`SetLocalHuman`) happens between screens; `ExchangeLocalStarFilm` keeps
    each star's prepared film. The command log records a switch as a Local
    record (type 5).
  - One log per human: `tools/split_commands.py`; `--replay-commands` takes a
    list or a directory and merges them in `Session.Humans` order between
    shared records, checking the barrier before each player-star job.
  - `tools/test.py`: `--sim-hotseat` with 2 and 8 humans, recorded, split, and
    replayed from the per-human logs (reverse order) to the same final hash; with
    2 humans a log without its end turns must trip the barrier.
- Notes:
  - Not covered by the barrier: the old hyperspace arcade, planet battles, text
    quests, new-game opening days. Film-end steps run for the human who watched
    the film. Humans who were not local do not see the day's film.
  - The UI could not be driven from the session; see docs/humans.md for what
    the hot-seat screens do, and the report for what to check by hand.
