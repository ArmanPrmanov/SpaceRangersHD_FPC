- Notes:
  - **A loaded game drifts from the live one it was saved from.** Nothing
    here fixes this for saves.
    - The original save format rounds some Doubles to Singles (equipment
      condition, the captain's health) and leaves out caches (ship wealth
      and strength). Checkpoints carry those exact values.
    - Divergence still appears 6–30 days after a reload, from further
      caches: new ship ids, planet fields.
    - So checkpoints are approximate. The replay reports hash differences
      after a checkpoint as `drift`, apart from `diverged`.
    - A recording that starts from a loaded save replays exactly, because
      the replay loads the same file.
